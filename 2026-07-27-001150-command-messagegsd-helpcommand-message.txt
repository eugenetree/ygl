● GSD Core — Git. Ship. Done.

  Plan-driven development for solo agentic work with Claude Code. GSD Core turns
  a vague idea into a hierarchical plan, then executes it phase by phase with
  state tracking and atomic commits.

  Start here (3 commands)

  roadmap

  planning

  /gsd-execute-phase 1    # Execute all plans in the phase

  initialize planning safely.
