# YouTube Captions Scraper

Discovers YouTube channels and their videos, scrapes the videos' captions, and makes them searchable.

## Language

### Prioritisation

**Boost**:
A permanent priority bump given to a channel, which ranks its jobs above everything else in every scraping queue.
_Avoid_: Push (a push results in a boost, but they are not the same thing)

**Push**:
A one-off manual request to bring a channel's details and video list up to date now, ahead of the backlog; it also boosts the channel.
_Avoid_: Boost, prioritize

### Channels

**Channel name**:
The name a channel shows on YouTube, under its videos and on its page.
_Avoid_: Username, handle (the `@` identifier in the channel's URL, a different thing)

### Scraping

**Skip**:
A video left unscraped because YouTube says why a viewer in the scraper's position (signed in, age not verified, in the scraper's country) can't watch it: members-only, Premium-only, age-restricted, removed by the uploader, and the like. It is a fact about the video, not about the scraper.
_Avoid_: Skippable error

**Failure**:
A video that couldn't be scraped for any other reason: YouTube refused the scraper, something on our side broke, or YouTube didn't say why the video can't be watched. It may be the video's fault or the scraper's.
_Avoid_: Unskippable error, error

### Videos

**Spoken language**:
The language actually spoken in a video's original audio. It is a fact about the video, independent of which captions YouTube happens to offer for it.
_Avoid_: Caption language

**Video language**:
The spoken language we record for a video, decided from YouTube's own labels. It can be wrong when they are.
_Avoid_: yt-dlp language (yt-dlp's top-level language follows the format it would download, not the video)

**Original audio**:
The audio track carrying the original mark, as opposed to its dubs. When a video has one, its language becomes the video language. That language can be mislabelled (e.g. the uploader set the wrong language in YouTube Studio), so it can differ from the spoken language.
_Avoid_: "original" for `-orig` auto-captions (those are YouTube's transcript of some audio track, not necessarily the original audio)

**Original mark**:
YouTube's label on an audio track saying it is the one the video was published with. Mostly dubbed videos carry one.
_Avoid_: Default audio (YouTube's other label, for the track it plays to this viewer)

**Dub**:
An extra audio track in another language, added alongside the original audio.
_Avoid_: Translation, audio track (too general)

### Search

**Caption**:
One timed piece of a video's captions, with a start, an end, its text, and whether it was written by a person (manual) or generated by YouTube (auto). A manual caption is a whole line; an auto caption is a single word.
_Avoid_: Subtitle, line, segment

**Caption track**:
All of a video's captions of one kind (auto or manual) in one language. A video we keep captions for has two: its auto track and its manual track.
_Avoid_: Subtitles, caption file

**Similarity score**:
The share of a video's manual caption words that its auto captions say at about the same time. It is how far we trust the manual captions to match what is actually spoken.
_Avoid_: Match rate, accuracy

**Searchable**:
A video is searchable when its auto and manual captions are both valid and its similarity score reaches the search threshold. Only a searchable video's manual captions are searched.
_Avoid_: Indexed, synced (those describe the search index catching up, not whether the video qualifies)

**Search**:
A word or phrase looked up across every searchable caption, answered with clips.
_Avoid_: Find, lookup, query (the query is the text being searched for, not the search)

**Clip**:
A caption as it comes back from a search: the video it is in (with the video's title and channel name), the caption's own start and end, its text, and a play-from point. It is a caption in the role of a result, not something stored separately.
_Avoid_: Hit, result, match

**Play-from point**:
The moment a clip should start playing, set slightly before the caption begins so the listener hears the phrase in context.
_Avoid_: Start time (that is the caption's own start), offset, lead-in

### Raw data

**Raw caption track**:
A caption track exactly as YouTube served it, before we parse it into captions. Kept only for videos whose auto and manual tracks we both fetched.
_Avoid_: Raw captions, subtitle file, json3

**Raw video info**:
Everything yt-dlp reported about a video the last time we fetched it, exactly as it returned it (metadata, the list of caption tracks, the audio formats), or the error it gave instead. Kept for every video we fetch, whether it was saved, skipped or failed.
_Avoid_: yt-dlp response, info JSON, dump, metadata
